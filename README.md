# UoPeople BS Health Science — fastest route with a $1,000 budget

**Updated 16 October 2026.** For students whose UoPeople assessment fees are covered by a full scholarship and who can spend up to US$1,000 on external credit and related costs.

**Read the [updated course and spending plan](FASTEST-1000-USD-GUIDE.md).** It includes provider mappings, a prerequisite-aware timetable, registration dates, and the questions that determine whether an earlier finish is possible.

**Potentially faster route found in further research:** UPI Study lists Health Psychology and Biology II equivalents. If UoPeople confirms these, resolves the biology credit/lab mismatch, approves an 83-credit transfer allocation and permits January prerequisite use, a four-term model could finish around **August 2027**, or **November 2027** with research capstones/later internship. These are conditional scenarios; the mappings have not been independently confirmed by UoPeople. See [the UPI option and its conditions](FASTEST-1000-USD-GUIDE.md#0-potentially-faster-upi-route--confirmation-required) before paying.

The Study.com/Sophia fallback budget is **two months of Study.com Pro + one optional month of Sophia + the biology lab: $644.67 before transfer fees, tax and transcript costs**. Keep the remaining **$355.33** available until those charges are known. Two months is a completion target, not a promise. The conditional UPI mix is costed separately in the guide.

Study.com now advertises **unlimited monthly course completions**, with up to three courses active on Pro. The old guide's two included exams and paid extra-exam calculation is obsolete. Pro is $235/month. [Current pricing](https://study.com/college/pricing.html)

For the Study.com/Sophia fallback with Online Education Strategies and Introduction to Health Science in November 2026, the modeled finish is **around January 2028 in the best case**, or **spring 2028 with research capstones or a later internship**. Moving Health Psychology into November could advance that by one term if approved. The degree audit, transfer timing and course availability control the actual plan.

The previous schedule had prerequisite errors. Its fixed 11-month and 16-month promises have been removed. Several published course codes also disagree across UoPeople's pathway PDF and current catalog; the new guide identifies those conflicts before any purchase.

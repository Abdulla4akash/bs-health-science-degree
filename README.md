# UoPeople BS Health Science — Maximum Savings Speedrun Guide

A step-by-step playbook for completing the **University of the People Bachelor of Science in Health Science (122 credits)** as fast and as cheaply as possible, using free CLEP exams, cheap transfer-credit providers, and UoPeople's own published transfer pathways.

> **Bottom line:** with the right stack you can knock ~73 of 122 credits out externally for **$100–$1,100 total** (depending on how fast you want to go) and finish the remaining ~16–24 courses at UoPeople in **4–7 terms**.

*Researched August 2026. Transfer guides referenced are valid 2025–2028. Always verify against the current [UoPeople catalog](https://catalog.uopeople.edu/) and your own transfer evaluation — mappings and prices change.*

## The key numbers (know these first)

| Fact | Value |
|---|---|
| Total credits required | **122** (min. 39 courses; most are 3cr, BIOL 1121/1122 are 4cr, internship is 6cr) |
| Max transfer credits | **90** (in practice the curriculum, not the cap, is the limit — ~73cr is the realistic external max) |
| Proctored courses required | **11** (the courses you must take at UoPeople cover this automatically) |
| Terms per year | **5** (9 weeks each) |
| Course load, Foundations (term 1) | **max 2 courses** |
| Course load, degree-seeking | **max 4/term with CGPA ≥ 3.00**; 1/term if CGPA < 2.00 or on probation |
| Quantitative Reasoning | **"Select 2"** — College Algebra + Statistics is enough. **You do not need Calculus.** |
| Capstone | HS 3995 Internship (6cr, one term, 270 supervised hours) **or** HS 4990 + HS 4991 (two terms) |

## The provider stack, cheapest first

| Provider | Cost | What it's for |
|---|---|---|
| **[Modern States](https://modernstates.org/) (CLEP)** | **FREE** — finish their free online course, they pay the CLEP exam fee *and* proctoring | Gen eds + unlimited elective credit |
| **[Sophia Learning](https://partneruopeople.sophia.org/)** | $99/month, 2 active courses at a time, **no exam cap** (watch for frequent promos) | Statistics, Nutrition, likely A&P and Ethics, elective top-ups |
| **[UPI Study](https://www.upistudy.com/pages/uopeople)** | $89/month or $279/4 months, unlimited | *Possible* cheap substitute for Study.com — **must confirm mappings first** (Step 3) |
| **[Study.com](https://www.uopeople.edu/about/partners/academic-partnerships/uopeople-partners-with-study-com/)** | $235/month, only **2 proctored exams included** (+up to 3 more at $70 each = max 5/month) | The ~8 courses nothing cheaper covers |
| **UoPeople itself** | Assessment fee ~$140–180/course (waived if you hold a **full scholarship** — then these courses are effectively **free**, they just cost *time*) | Everything else |

**Golden rule:** if you have a UoPeople scholarship, external credit never saves you money — it only saves you *time*. Buy speed only where it matters.

**Transfer fee note:** Sophia and Study.com each waive transfer fees for up to 20 courses; beyond waivers UoPeople charges ~$17/course transferred.

## Step 0 — Apply to UoPeople and submit transcripts FIRST

Before paying anyone anything:

1. Apply to UoPeople (BS Health Science) and submit **all existing transcripts** (including any prior degree — a prior bachelor's/master's may clear gen-ed slots for free).
2. UoPeople's Transfer Evaluation team responds within ~72 hours.
3. **Why first:** every official pathway PDF warns to get evaluated *before* taking external courses, so you don't pay for credit you already have or that won't apply — duplicated coursework doesn't double-count.

Cost so far: **$0** (application/assessment handled by scholarship if you have one; otherwise budget the ~$60 application fee).

## Step 1 — Modern States CLEP grind (FREE, start immediately)

Register at [modernstates.org](https://modernstates.org/). For each exam: complete their free online course → request the exam + proctoring voucher → sit the CLEP.

Per the [official UoPeople↔Modern States Health Science pathway](https://www.uopeople.edu/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Modern-States-Partner-Launch_HS.pdf) and the [BA pathway](https://www.uopeople.edu/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Modern-States-Partner-Launch_BA.pdf), take these:

| CLEP exam | Clears at UoPeople |
|---|---|
| College Composition | ENGL 1102 English Composition 2 |
| College Algebra | MATH 1201 |
| Western Civilization I *or* II | HIST 1421 Greek and Roman Civilization |
| Introductory Sociology | SOC 1502 (major prerequisite!) |
| Two of: American Lit / Analyzing & Interpreting Lit / English Lit / US History I / US History II / Humanities | Humanities requirement (6cr) |
| **Any additional CLEP exams** (Psychology, American Government, Biology, Chemistry, Marketing, Management…) | **Elective credit** — farm the ~18–21cr elective block here for free |

That's ~10–12 free exams ≈ **~33–39 credits for $0**. Pace: 1–2 exams/week is a common grind rate; a couple of months of evenings.

## Step 2 — One surgical month of Sophia ($99–$198)

Sophia covers what CLEP can't. Subscribe for one month (two if needed), run two courses at a time, cancel when done:

| Sophia course | Clears at UoPeople | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Introduction to Statistics | MATH 1280 (there is **no CLEP statistics exam**) | Solid — on the [official mapping](https://www.uopeople.edu/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/Sophia-Recommended-Course-List.pdf) |
| Introduction to Nutrition (NUTR101) | **HS 2611 Nutrition** — a major core course | Solid — on the official mapping |
| Anatomy & Physiology I or II (BIO201/202) | **HS 2211 Human Anatomy & Physiology** — a major core course | Likely — cited in UoPeople's own [Modern States HS pathway PDF](https://www.uopeople.edu/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Modern-States-Partner-Launch_HS.pdf); **confirm in Step 3** |
| Introduction to Ethics | PHIL 1404 Ethics and Social Responsibility | Likely — listed on [Sophia's partner page](https://partneruopeople.sophia.org/); **confirm in Step 3** |
| Any remaining elective gaps | Elective credit | Solid |

Notes: Sophia grades don't enter your UoPeople GPA (protects the 3.00 you need for 4 courses/term), and Sophia enrollment doesn't count as UoPeople enrollment — run it in parallel with anything.

## Step 3 — One email that can save you ~$700

Before buying Study.com, email UoPeople Transfer Evaluation and ask them to confirm, in writing:

1. Does **Sophia BIO201/202 (Anatomy & Physiology)** satisfy **HS 2211**?
2. Does **Sophia Introduction to Ethics** satisfy **PHIL 1404**?
3. Which **UPI Study** courses map to: **HS 1101, COM 2001, HS 2212, HS 4241, HS 4810, HS 3810, HS 4812, BIOL 1121**? (UPI's published equivalencies — e.g. Healthcare 120 Medical Terminology → intro health science, Healthcare 302 → healthcare management — use older UoPeople course codes, so get current-catalog confirmation.)
4. Does your scholarship cover per-course assessment fees and transfer fees?

They answer within ~72 hours. If UPI Study covers even half of the Study.com-exclusive list, you replace a ~$700–1,100 spend with **$89–$279**.

## Step 4 — Close the gap: the ~8 courses only money can transfer

After Steps 1–2, exactly these remain externally coverable **only** via Study.com (per the [official Study.com HS pathway](https://www.uopeople.edu/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/studycom-uopeople-degree-transfer-pathways-health-science.pdf)) — unless Step 3 unlocked UPI:

| UoPeople course | Study.com course |
|---|---|
| BIOL 1121 Biology I (4cr) | Biology 101 + Biology 101L (lab) |
| HS 1101 Intro to Health Science | Health 101: Principles of Health |
| COM 2001 Professional Communication | Business 113 / Business 313 / Communications 102 |
| HS 2212 Infectious Diseases | Biology 103: Microbiology *or* Biology 106: Pathophysiology |
| HS 4241 Psychopathology & Mental Health | Psychology 306: Advanced Abnormal Psychology |
| HS 4810 Health Policy & Management | Health 302 *or* Health 303 |
| HS 3810 Health Systems (program elective) | Health 307: Healthcare Delivery Systems |
| HS 4812 Bioethics (program elective) | Health 301: Ethical & Legal Issues in Healthcare |

**Pick your scenario:**

| Scenario | Study.com spend | Total all-in | Extra UoPeople courses | Finish (Sept start, max load) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A — Skip it entirely**, take all 8 at UoPeople | $0 | **~$100–200** | +8 (≈ 24 total) | **~7 terms (~16 months)** |
| **B — One month, 5 exams** (2 included + 3×$70): Biology 101(+L), Health 101, Business 113, Biology 103, Health 302 | ~$445 | **~$550–650** | +3 (≈ 19 total) | **~6 terms (~13 months)** |
| **C — Two months, all 8** | ~$820 | **~$950–1,100** | 0 (≈ 16 total) | **~5 terms (~11 months)** |

Study.com strategy if you buy it: **batch, don't trickle.** Prep everything, subscribe, blitz 4–5 exams/month, cancel. Paying $235/month to take 2 exams is the trap.

## Step 5 — The UoPeople terms (free with scholarship; plan the sequencing)

Whatever scenario you chose, roughly this remains at UoPeople: UNIV 1001, PSYC 1111, BIOL 1122 (4cr), HS 2711, HS 2712, HS 3311 Epidemiology, HS 3610, HS 4212 Genetics, HS 4510 Biostatistics, HS 2720, HS 3210, HS 3814, CHEM 3212, one elective, capstone — plus whatever you didn't buy in Step 4.

Sequencing rules that actually bind:

1. **Term 1 = Foundations = 2 courses max.** Take UNIV 1001 + PSYC 1111 (or HS 1101 if you skipped Study.com).
2. **Keep CGPA ≥ 3.00** from day one — it's the key to 4 courses/term from term 2 onward.
3. **Front-load the capstone gate:** capstone/internship eligibility requires 80+ credits *and* the 10 core courses done. With transfers, your UoPeople-side gate courses are BIOL 1122, HS 2711, HS 2712, HS 3311, HS 3610, HS 4510 — schedule them in terms 2–4, not last.
4. Respect prereq chains (BIOL 1121→1122, HS 2711→2712).
5. **Capstone choice:** HS 3995 Internship = one term but 30 hrs/week for 9 weeks; HS 4990+HS 4991 = two terms but course-shaped. If you'll be starting a PhD or job, the research route runs quieter in the background; if you want the earliest finish date and can spare a free summer term, the internship is one term shorter.

Example max-speed schedule (Scenario C, September start):

| Term | Courses |
|---|---|
| T1 (Sept–Nov) | UNIV 1001, PSYC 1111 |
| T2 (Nov–Jan) | BIOL 1122, HS 2711, HS 3610, HS 2720 |
| T3 (Feb–Apr) | HS 2712, HS 3311, HS 4212, HS 3210 |
| T4 (Apr–Jun) | HS 4510, CHEM 3212, HS 3814, elective |
| T5 (Jun–Aug) | Capstone (internship route) |

## Step 6 — Transfer logistics and pitfalls

- **Batch your transcripts.** Send Sophia/CLEP/Study.com transcripts as you complete each provider's block, and always *before* registering for a UoPeople course that might duplicate them.
- **Fee waivers:** first 20 Sophia courses and first 20 Study.com courses transfer free; ~$17/course beyond that.
- **External courses ≠ UoPeople enrollment** — no scholarship coverage, no GPA impact, no enrollment status. All upside for a speedrun.
- **Proctoring at UoPeople:** you can nominate a free personal proctor for proctored finals; paid online proctoring is the convenient fallback (~$10–20/exam). Budget $0–250.
- **Don't over-transfer:** stop external courses once your remaining plan is covered — credits beyond 122 are wasted money/time (and capped at 90 anyway).
- **Get everything in writing** from the transfer evaluation team before paying for any course whose mapping isn't on an official pathway PDF.

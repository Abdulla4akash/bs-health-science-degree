# Scholarship route without paid external subscriptions

**Revised 16 October 2026.** This companion covers the decision to avoid Study.com, Sophia and other paid course subscriptions. For the fastest paid route, use the [current $1,000 guide](FASTEST-1000-USD-GUIDE.md).

With UoPeople assessment fees covered by a scholarship, taking more courses at UoPeople can minimize spending. It also leaves more courses in the university's prerequisite sequence. A scholarship does not by itself establish coverage for outside providers, credential evaluations, transcript delivery or transfer charges.

## Start with credits already earned

Review the existing degree audit and previous transfer decisions before repeating general education courses. Overseas university credits can require a paid course-by-course evaluation; compare its actual cost and processing time with the credit it would remove. Follow the current [transfer policy](https://www.uopeople.edu/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Transfer-Credit-Policy-09022026.pdf).

## Treat CLEP as an optional route to investigate

UoPeople publishes a [Modern States Health Science pathway](https://www.uopeople.edu/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Modern-States-Partner-Launch_HS.pdf). Use it to identify possible general education equivalents, then check the current [Modern States](https://modernstates.org/) voucher terms, [College Board CLEP](https://clep.collegeboard.org/) testing eligibility in your location, test-center availability and reimbursement conditions before relying on a zero-cost exam.

This revision does **not** certify current voucher eligibility, international remote-testing access, reimbursement or a particular number of free credits. A general elective only helps if the degree audit still has space for it. Do not count an exam twice or assume introductory psychology replaces Health Psychology.

## Build the university timetable from prerequisites

Use the [current Health Science course catalog](https://catalog.uopeople.edu/ug_term1_item/health-science/courses-in-health-science), then have the adviser reconcile it with the student's applicable catalog and degree audit. Courses that depend on one another must occupy successive terms unless UoPeople expressly approves an exception.

The old seven-term schedule incorrectly placed Biology II with courses that require it and Epidemiology with Genetics. It has been withdrawn. The former claims of roughly 39 guaranteed free credits, a guaranteed 16-month completion and a $500 maximum cost are also withdrawn.

The [paid guide's timing discussion](FASTEST-1000-USD-GUIDE.md#4-the-university-timetable) explains the relevant chains and capstone application timing. Its accelerated timetable assumes accepted paid transfers and cannot be reused unchanged for this route.

## A practical spending rule

Keep paid subscriptions at $0 if that is the priority. Before each optional transfer, record the exact degree requirement it clears, all charges, and the term it would save. If it only replaces a scholarship-covered course that already fits into an otherwise spare slot, paying for it may provide no earlier completion date.

Confirm scholarship duration and remaining course coverage, then make a term plan from the actual audit. There is no verified universal zero-spend completion date.

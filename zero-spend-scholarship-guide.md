# The Zero-Spend Route — Full Scholarship + Free CLEP Only

**Who this is for:** you hold a full UoPeople scholarship (assessment fees covered) and refuse to pay for *any* external course provider — no Sophia, no Study.com, no UPI. The only money you'll allow is proctoring-related fees.

**Bottom line: ~$0–$500 total (realistically ~$100–300, and $0 is genuinely achievable), finishing in ~7 terms ≈ 16 months at maximum course load.**

*Companion to the [main guide](README.md), which covers the paid shortcuts. Researched August 2026 — verify against the current catalog and your transfer evaluation.*

---

## The stack

Only two components, both free:

1. **[Modern States](https://modernstates.org/) CLEP exams** — their free online course earns you a voucher covering the CLEP exam fee (and they have covered proctoring/test-center admin fees; policies vary, see costs below).
2. **UoPeople courses on scholarship** — assessment fees covered, so every course is free and the only budget is *time and term slots*.

## Step 1 — CLEP everything CLEP can touch (~39 credits, ~12 exams, $0)

From the [official UoPeople↔Modern States Health Science pathway](https://www.uopeople.edu/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Modern-States-Partner-Launch_HS.pdf):

| # | CLEP exam | Clears at UoPeople | Credits |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | College Composition | ENGL 1102 | 3 |
| 2 | College Algebra | MATH 1201 | 3 |
| 3 | Western Civilization I *or* II | HIST 1421 | 3 |
| 4 | Introductory Sociology | SOC 1502 (major prerequisite) | 3 |
| 5–6 | Two of: American Lit, Analyzing & Interpreting Lit, English Lit, US History I, US History II, Humanities | Humanities requirement | 6 |
| 7–13 | Any other CLEP exams — Introductory Psychology, American Government, Biology, Chemistry, Principles of Management, Principles of Marketing, Natural Sciences… | Elective block | ~18–21 |

Pacing: exams 1–6 before your first term if possible; the elective exams (7–13) can be knocked out *during* UoPeople terms — CLEP study runs fine alongside coursework, and elective credit can be transferred in as you go.

**What CLEP cannot cover** (no such exams exist): MATH 1280 Statistics, COM 2001, PHIL 1404 (as a named course), and every HS/BIOL major course. Those all go on the UoPeople side.

## Step 2 — Everything else at UoPeople (~83 credits, ~26 courses, $0 with scholarship)

The complete list you'll take at UoPeople:

**Gen ed / foundation (6 courses):** UNIV 1001 · MATH 1280 Statistics · COM 2001 · PHIL 1404 · PSYC 1111 · HS 1101

**Major spine (14 courses):** BIOL 1121 (4cr) · BIOL 1122 (4cr) · HS 2211 Anatomy & Physiology · HS 2212 Infectious Diseases · HS 2611 Nutrition · HS 2711 · HS 2712 · HS 3311 Epidemiology · HS 3610 · HS 4212 Genetics · HS 4241 Psychopathology · HS 4510 Biostatistics · HS 4810 Health Policy

**Program electives (6 courses):** HS 2720 Oral Health · HS 3210 Human Diseases · HS 3814 Community Health · HS 3810 Health Systems · HS 4812 Bioethics · CHEM 3212 Biochemistry

**Capstone (6cr):** HS 3995 Internship (one term, 270 hours) *or* HS 4990 + HS 4991 (two terms)

This comfortably clears the 11-proctored-course requirement — most of the HS spine is proctored anyway.

## The schedule — 7 terms at maximum load

Rules that bind: term 1 (Foundations) is capped at **2 courses**; from term 2 you need **CGPA ≥ 3.00** to carry **4 courses/term**; capstone eligibility needs **80+ credits and the 10 core courses**, so the gate courses go early. Example September start:

| Term | Courses (4/term after T1) |
|---|---|
| T1 (Sept–Nov) | UNIV 1001, HS 1101 |
| T2 (Nov–Jan) | BIOL 1121, PSYC 1111, MATH 1280, PHIL 1404 |
| T3 (Feb–Apr) | BIOL 1122, HS 2211, HS 2611, COM 2001 |
| T4 (Apr–Jun) | HS 2711, HS 2212, HS 3610, HS 2720 |
| T5 (Jun–Aug) | HS 2712, HS 3311, HS 4212, HS 3210 |
| T6 (Sept–Nov) | HS 4510, HS 4810, HS 4241, HS 3814 |
| T7 (Nov–Jan) | Capstone (internship route) + HS 3810, HS 4812, CHEM 3212 |

≈ **16 months start to finish.** Slack scenarios: research-capstone route or any term at 3 courses pushes into T8 (~+2 months). CLEP elective exams run in parallel throughout.

*(Check each course's actual term availability when registering — not every course runs every term; swap within the same tier where needed, keeping the T2–T5 gate courses early.)*

## The complete cost table

| Item | Cost |
|---|---|
| Modern States CLEP exam fees (~12 exams) | **$0** — voucher covers the exam fee |
| CLEP proctoring/test-center admin fees | **$0–25/exam** — many centers charge nothing; Modern States has run proctoring reimbursement; CLEP's at-home online option carries a proctoring fee if you choose it |
| UoPeople application + assessment fees (~26 courses) | **$0** — scholarship (verify yours covers assessment fees **in writing**) |
| UoPeople proctored finals (~12–15 of your courses) | **$0** with a personal proctor (a librarian, teacher, or other approved professional you nominate — free); ~$10–20/exam if you opt for paid online proctoring instead |
| Transfer evaluation / transfer fees | $0 evaluation; ~$17/course only where waivers don't apply — CLEP-via-partner should be free, confirm |
| **Total** | **$0 is achievable; ~$100–300 typical; ~$500 absolute worst case** |

**The two habits that keep it at $0:** pick a CLEP test center with no admin fee (call ahead), and set up a personal proctor with UoPeople in term 1 so you never pay per-exam proctoring for 15 finals.

## What the paid shortcuts would have bought you

| Route | Total cost | Finish |
|---|---|---|
| **This guide (zero-spend)** | ~$0–300 | ~16 months (~7 terms) |
| + Sophia month ([main guide](README.md) Scenario A) | ~$100–450 | ~16 months but 2 fewer UoPeople courses of load |
| + Study.com 1 month (Scenario B) | ~$550–900 | ~13 months |
| + Study.com 2 months (Scenario C) | ~$950–1,250 | ~11 months |

So versus the fastest paid route, zero-spend costs you **~5 extra months** and a heavier per-term load — and saves ~$1,000. If those months don't collide with anything (a job, a PhD, a life event), this is the rational choice: the marginal $1,000 buys speed you may not need.

## Honest caveats

- The 16-month figure assumes **4 courses/term every term with CGPA ≥ 3.00** and course availability lining up. Miss either and it stretches by a term at a time. 4 UoPeople courses = ~35–45 hrs/week — this is a full-time job.
- Scholarship terms differ. Confirm yours covers **assessment fees for all courses** and has no minimum/maximum pace conditions.
- Course-to-term availability isn't guaranteed; build each term's registration list one term ahead with an advisor.
- Only the transfer evaluation team's written answer makes any mapping real — including every CLEP row above.
